Who we are

A studio, not a factory.

Fawry Media brings together graphic designers, brand strategists, interface designers and front-end engineers in one small, focused team. We take on a limited number of projects at a time so that every one of them gets senior attention from kick-off to launch.

Our work spans two closely connected disciplines. In Creative Design we shape identities, campaigns, print and product interfaces. In Web Development we translate those systems into fast, accessible, hand-coded websites. Clients come to us for one and usually stay for both — because the hand-off between design and build simply disappears.

We are deliberately framework-agnostic and performance-obsessed. We don't reach for heavy tooling when clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript will produce a lighter, longer-lasting result.

01

Creative philosophy

Every element should earn its place. If a shape, a word or a line of code doesn't serve the idea, it goes.

We believe restraint is a creative act. The brands that last are the ones built on a single, clear thought that can stretch across a business card and a 4K screen without losing its character. Our job is to find that thought and give it a form that is impossible to confuse with anyone else's.

On the web, we apply the same discipline to code. Every kilobyte, animation and interaction must justify itself in terms of clarity, speed or delight. The result is work that feels effortless to use precisely because so much effort went into deciding what to leave out.
